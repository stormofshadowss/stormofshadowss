Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

StormOfShadowss is a small group-order service run by one person (the GOM — "we"). We decide what is done with the information below and are responsible for looking after it.

Questions or requests about your information: get in touch with the GOM the same way you normally order (for example on Instagram).

What we keep, and why

  • Your Instagram handle — so your claims, payments and parcels are tied to you.
  • Your email address — to sign you in with a one-time link, and to send order emails only if you turn them on.
  • Delivery details (name, address, email, phone) — only if you add them, so we can post your parcels.
  • Your orders and payments — what you claimed, what you owe and have paid, when, how, and any credit on your account.
  • Parcel preferences — an optional bias, personalised Lomo name, packing notes and saved defaults.
  • Sign-in security details — when you sign in we record the type of device or browser and the IP address with your session, to keep accounts safe.

We use it to run the group orders (taking claims, tracking payments, arranging shipping), to email you if you ask us to, to keep accounts and payment records, and to keep the site secure. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for marketing.

Cookies

Two small cookies, both needed for the site to work: one keeps you signed in, and one remembers which browser made a claim so you can link your handle to your email. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and the pages you order on don't load anything from other companies' servers.

The site also keeps a few things in your browser's own storage so it behaves the way you'd expect: your basket while you shop, the Instagram handle you last used (so you don't have to retype it), and whether you chose light or dark mode. These stay on your device — your basket and handle only reach us when you submit a claim, and your light/dark choice never does.

Who else sees it

Only the GOM can see your details. Your delivery name, address and phone number are passed to the postal or courier service only so they can deliver your parcel. Sign-in links and order emails are sent through our email provider. The site runs on a server we control.

Why we're allowed to

To run your orders and parcels (that is the service you asked for); our legitimate interest in running the service safely and keeping it fair; a legal duty to keep accounting records; and your consent for the optional order emails, which you can switch off at any time.

How long we keep it

While you have an account, so you can see your orders.

You can delete your account at any time under My orders → My settings. (We can't do it while you still owe money, a payment is waiting to be checked, or a parcel is on its way.) When you do, your sign-in, email, delivery details, bias and Lomo names and parcel notes are deleted.

If you have never had a confirmed order or a payment, everything about you is deleted. If you have, we keep a record of the order and payment — what, how much and when — without your name, because we need to keep accounting records. We keep those for as long as accounting, tax and dispute rules require.

Two exceptions: if you leave with credit still on your account we keep your Instagram handle so we can settle it with you, and a blocked handle is kept so the block continues to work.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete what we hold about you. You can change your delivery details yourself under Delivery details, change your sign-in email under My settings, and delete your account there too. For anything else — for example a copy of your information — use the contact above.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top.
